Our Board

The people behind Leave Your Mark Foundation.

Mark Dorris Jr.
Founder & Executive Director
Former collegiate athlete and 12 year professional basketball player in Europe
▾

Mark Dorris Jr. founded Leave Your Mark Foundation to honor the life and legacy of his father, Mark Dorris Sr., and to ensure that no child misses an opportunity because of financial barriers. After a 12 year professional basketball career across Europe, Mark returned home with a mission to use sports as a vehicle for mentorship, opportunity, and lasting change. He believes every child deserves a coach, a mentor, and someone who believes in them. Through LYMF, he is committed to breaking down financial barriers, empowering the next generation, and inspiring communities to leave a positive mark that extends far beyond the game.

Zena Vaughn
President
Principal, Cincinnati Achievement Academy
▾

"I have spent my life believing that every child deserves someone who believes in them and every opportunity to discover their potential." With more than 40 years of experience in education, leadership, advocacy, and human resources, Zena Vaughn has dedicated a career to creating opportunities that empower children, strengthen families, and build thriving communities. Her professional journey has included teaching students from Pre K through 12, serving as human resources manager at Hamilton County Department of Education, and advocating for equitable access to education and community resources. As President of the Board, Zena is committed to advancing the organization's mission and is honored to help build a lasting legacy that ensures every child has the opportunity to participate, belong, and thrive.

Latrice Watkins
Secretary
Student Success Coordinator & Academic Advisor, University of Cincinnati
▾

Latrice is an educator by passion and profession, shaped by years of working with underprivileged and underrepresented youth. As a former schoolteacher and proud product of interscholastic and intercollegiate sports, she brings a deep commitment to youth development, mentorship, and the belief that early opportunities can change lifelong trajectories. Her extensive clerical and organizational skills strengthen her ability to support strategic initiatives and operational needs. Latrice joined the board to help expand access, amplify young voices, and ensure every child has the chance to grow, thrive, and succeed in their own legacy.

Jontae' Johnson
Treasurer
Director of Human Resources, Warrensville Heights
▾

Jontae' M. Johnson is an accomplished leader in education, human resources, and nonprofit management with extensive experience in organizational leadership, governance, and strategic planning. She holds a Master of Nonprofit Organizations from Case Western Reserve University's Mandel School and is passionate about building strong organizations that expand opportunities and create lasting impact for children, families, and communities. As both a nonprofit professional and a mother of four daughters, Jontae' believes every child deserves access to mentorship, encouragement, and opportunities to thrive, regardless of their circumstances. She is honored to serve on the Board of LYMF, helping to build a sustainable, high impact organization that carries forward the legacy of Mark Dorris Sr. while creating pathways for the next generation to leave their mark.

Abbey Cummins
Director of Community Outreach
Founder, Everything Cincy
▾

Abbey is passionate about building connections, supporting local organizations, and creating opportunities that leave a lasting impact. As the founder of Everything Cincy, she has spent her career focused on storytelling, marketing, community engagement, and helping businesses and organizations connect with the Cincinnati community in meaningful ways. Through her work, she has collaborated with local businesses, nonprofits, and community leaders, gaining a deeper appreciation for the people and organizations that make the region special. Abbey joined the Leave Your Mark board because she believes in the importance of giving back and using her experience to help support a mission that creates meaningful change.
