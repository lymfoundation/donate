Why It Matters
Potential Doesn't Care
About a Paycheck

Mentorship changes the trajectory of a child's life, on the court and off it. The skills learned through sports and consistent coaching don't stay in the gym. They shape how a young person handles pressure, builds character, and leads. LYMF exists to make sure that access to those experiences is determined by potential, not by a family's financial situation.

Why I Started This
From the Founder

I was ten years old when I lost my father, Mark Dorris Sr., on Thanksgiving Day in 1997. I was lost, and basketball found me. It truly saved my life, gave me structure and purpose, took me through college, and allowed me to travel the world playing the game professionally for 12 years.

With the rising cost of youth sports, many children with real ability miss their opportunity for reasons completely outside their control. Leave Your Mark Foundation exists to bridge that gap and grant access, so that a family's financial situation never determines a child's potential.

We aim to leave our mark now, so they can leave their mark later and make the world a better place.

Mark Dorris
Founder, Leave Your Mark Foundation
The Legacy
Mark Dorris Sr.
In Loving Memory

This foundation carries forward the values Mark Dorris Sr. demonstrated throughout his life: service, mentorship, integrity, and community leadership. Everything LYMF does is an extension of the mark he left on the people around him. The foundation bears his name so that legacy is never forgotten.

"Mark Dorris Sr. was the kind of man who made everyone around him feel seen, supported, and valued. He led by example, in his home, in his community, and in how he gave of himself to others. His spirit lives on in every child this foundation reaches."

— Kerry Kane Miller, in tribute to Mark Dorris Sr.
