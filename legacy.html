Mark Dorris Sr. 1958–1997

Before the Foundation,
there was a man.

A husband. A father. A mentor.

This foundation carries his name because he carried everyone else's weight first.

Mark Dorris Sr. grew up in Cincinnati and picked up basketball later than most of his peers — but by his senior year at Hughes High School, he'd drawn offers from programs like Tennessee, UNLV, Dayton, and Xavier. He chose to stay home and play for the Bearcats.

His path at the University of Cincinnati wasn't instant. He worked his way from spot minutes as an underclassman to a 22-game starter as a junior, then into a senior season where he became the heartbeat of the team — leading in scoring, rebounding, and shooting percentage, and logging more minutes than anyone in the league. That season earned him All-Metro Conference honors.

After a stint playing professionally in Austria, he came home and gave back. He spent the rest of his life as a mentor and youth basketball coach, and worked directly with Cincinnati families and children through Job & Family Services, Vine Elementary, and Porter Middle School — including teaching "Parent Power," a class that helped parents show up for their children's education.

Above all, he was a great husband to Annette Dorris and a great father to Jontae', Mark, and Michael Dorris.

Basketball gave him his start, and basketball is where he stayed until the end. On Thanksgiving Day, 1997, he suffered a massive heart attack on a basketball court, doing the thing that had shaped his entire life. The game was never just a game to him. It was where he showed up, where he built relationships, where he coached and mentored — and it's where he was standing the last time his heart beat.

Leave Your Mark Foundation exists because of what he taught before there was ever a name for it: that talent is everywhere, but someone has to build the bridge to opportunity. That's his legacy. This is us keeping it going.
